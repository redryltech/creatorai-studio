## Key Design Principles

1. One command creates everything
2. Zero clicks gets it published
3. Real-time visibility into AI work
4. First value within 3.5 minutes of signup
5. AI suggestions proactively guide creators
6. Every interaction saves time vs manual workflow

## Unique Features Proposed

- **Content DNA** — AI extracts your personal content formula from your best-performing videos
- **Ghost Mode** — Fully autonomous content creation and publishing on autopilot
- **Split Universe** — Automatic A/B variation generation across hooks, styles, and thumbnails
- **Audience Simulator** — Predicted performance before publishing
- **Trend Radar** — Live niche-relevant trend dashboard with one-click creation
- **Content Remix** — Transform any content format into any other format
